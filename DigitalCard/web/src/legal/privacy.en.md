# Privacy Policy

> **Draft.** To be reviewed by a lawyer before publication.

- **Collected:** account name, e-mail, phone; card content; details visitors leave **with consent**; anonymous statistics; payment references (no card numbers).
- **Not collected:** visitors' IP addresses are never stored. Unique visitors are counted with a one-way hash using a salt that changes daily (kept 13 months).
- Signed-in visitors are shown by name to card owners **only** if they enable "Show my name to card owners".
- Processors: Supabase (database), QPay (payments), Cloudflare (spam protection), an e-mail provider. We never sell your data.
- You can view, edit, export (CSV, .vcf) and delete your data and account.
