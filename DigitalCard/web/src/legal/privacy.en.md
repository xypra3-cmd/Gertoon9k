# Privacy Policy

> **Draft.** To be reviewed by a lawyer under Mongolia's Law on Personal Data Protection (in force since 1 May 2022) before publication.

Controller: [Company] LLC, [address], [privacy@digitalcard.mn].

- **Collected:** account name, e-mail, phone; card content and images; contacts you keep (left by visitors **with consent**, added or scanned by you), notes and follow-ups; the **public** key of your passkeys; a ~1 km area only while you use «Bump» (kept 10 minutes); anonymous statistics; payment references (no card numbers).
- **Not collected:** visitors' IP addresses are never stored (unique visitors are counted with a one-way hash whose salt changes daily, kept 13 months); no precise or background location; no ad tracking.
- **On your device:** offline business-card scanning (the photo is not uploaded), writing your card link to NFC tags, home/lock-screen widgets.
- **Processors and transfers abroad:** Supabase (database, Singapore), Anthropic/Claude (only when you press an AI button; not used for training, USA), QPay (payments, Mongolia), Cloudflare Turnstile (spam protection), Resend (e-mail, USA), Apple/Google Wallet (only when you add a pass). You give explicit consent to this storage and processing abroad when you sign up and can withdraw it by deleting your account. We never sell your data.
- Signed-in visitors are shown by name to card owners **only** if they enable "Show my name to card owners".
- **Your rights:** view, edit, export (CSV, .vcf), withdraw consent, delete your account. Complaints: [privacy@digitalcard.mn] or the National Human Rights Commission of Mongolia.
- Not for children under 16.
