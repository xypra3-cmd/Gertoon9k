# Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account and all data (cards, contacts, statistics) at any time.

- **In the app:** Settings → Delete account → Delete. Deletion is immediate.
- **On the web:** log in → Settings → Delete account.
- **Cannot log in?** E-mail [support@digitalcard.mn] from your account address with the subject "Delete account". We delete within 30 days.

Deleted: profile, cards, links, images, contacts, notes, statistics, passkeys. Widgets, NFC tags and Wallet passes live on your devices — remove them yourself (the card link then shows "not found"). Kept: payment and e-barimt records (tax law), anonymised audit entries. Organization owners must transfer ownership first.
